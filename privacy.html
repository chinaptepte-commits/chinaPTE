Information we use

When you choose to sign in with TikTok, we use the account information TikTok provides, such as your TikTok user identifier and display name, to create and maintain your sign-in session. We do not request your TikTok password.

We use information to provide sign-in, maintain the service, improve learning features, and support user-requested publishing to the user's own TikTok account. We do not sell personal information.

We retain information only as needed to operate the service. You may request deletion of your chinaPTE account data by contacting the chinaPTE site operator.

For privacy questions, contact the chinaPTE site operator through the website at chinapte.net.
